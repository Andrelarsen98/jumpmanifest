Is it jumpable today?

Manifest deliberately does not answer it with a green tick or a red cross.

Two hours at Skydive Voss, in the Manifest app.

The next few hours
A strip of hours from early morning to late evening, each with sky, temperature and wind, with sunrise and sunset dropped in where they fall.
The day in one line
“Rain around for parts of the day”, then the calmest window left in today’s daylight, such as “Calmest 19:00 to 21:00”.
Tap any hour
The whole screen moves with it: sky, winds at each height, cloud and the notes in between.
Sunrise and sunset
In the dropzone’s own local time, so you know how long the light lasts for a sunset load.

Whether a load flies depends on the aircraft, the dropzone’s cloud policy, the S&TA on duty and who is on the load. Some dropzones fly through conditions that hold others on the ground. Manifest gives you the numbers that decision is made from.

Your licence, your canopy and your currency set what you jump in. Not the forecast.

How it works

Check it before you drive.

Five steps from the App Store to the calmest window.

  1. Download Manifest.

    From the App Store, then set your home dropzone.

  2. Open Weather.

    The sky, the ground wind and gusts, and the next few hours at a glance.

  3. Read the three winds.

    Exit, canopy and ground, plus any turn or change between them.

  4. Scrub the hours.

    Find the calmest window, then check sunset.

  5. Open the full profile.

    Every 1,000 ft, and cloud by layer.

At the dropzone

On the desk.
On the hangar TV.

At dropzones running Manifest Desk, the desk’s top bar always shows the current ground wind, and the hangar board shows the wind, the temperature and sunset next to the loads and the calls.

With live loads and load calls there too, you know when you are going up as well as what it is like up there.

Your judgement first.

Weather in Manifest supports your judgement and the dropzone’s. It never replaces either.

A forecast model can miss a local gust front, a sea breeze or a cloud bank sitting over the field, and global models are weaker in complex terrain like fjords and mountains. Look at the sky, look at the wind sock, listen to the S&TA and the pilot, and stay within your own limits.

If the dropzone holds the loads, the loads are held.

Frequently asked questions

Does Manifest tell me if it is jumpable today?

No, on purpose. Whether a load flies is the dropzone’s decision and depends on the aircraft, cloud policy, the S&TA on duty and who is jumping. Manifest shows the wind at each height, cloud, rain and the calmest window, so you can judge it yourself.

Does the app show winds aloft for skydiving?

Yes. It shows the wind at 13,000 ft where you get out, around 3,000 ft under canopy and on the ground with gusts. The full profile lists the wind every 1,000 ft above the dropzone with direction, temperature and cloud.

Where does the weather data come from?

It is a forecast from the Open-Meteo weather model, for today and tomorrow. Everything on the screen, including Now, is a forecast, not a measurement at the field.

Can I check the weather at a dropzone that is not my home dropzone?

Yes. Pick any dropzone from the picker at the top of the weather screen, then tap once to go back to your home dropzone.

Can I see the wind in m/s or mph instead of knots?

Yes. Wind can be shown in knots, mph or m/s, heights in feet or metres, and temperature in Celsius or Fahrenheit.

Is the weather screen free?

Yes. Weather is part of the free Manifest app on iPhone.
