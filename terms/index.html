3. 3D Jump Track — recording, and what the numbers are worth

3D Jump Track records a jump using the sensors in your phone and replays it afterwards.

IT IS NOT AN ALTIMETER AND IT IS NOT A SAFETY DEVICE. Every altitude, speed and time it shows is derived, after the fact, from a consumer phone barometer and consumer GPS carried in a pocket. It must never be used for altitude awareness, for deciding when to deploy, or for any in-air decision whatsoever. Use your altimeter, your AAD and your eyes. Nothing the App reports about a jump changes what your instruments and your training tell you.

The numbers are for looking back at a jump, not for flying one. They may be wrong. GPS drops out in an aircraft, barometric altitude drifts with weather, and detection of exit, deployment and landing is inference rather than measurement. We do not warrant that any recorded figure is accurate, and you must not rely on one to establish what happened in an incident, an investigation, or a dispute.

Recording needs your permission for location (including in the background) and motion, and it only runs when you start it or when the load you are pinned to is called. Your tracks are stored on your device and are not uploaded to us; see the Privacy Policy.
