Questions? Answers.

What counts as a seat?

A jumper on a load that took off. Crew, cancelled loads and practice never count.

When are we charged?

Once a month, for the seats you flew the month before. A month with no flying is $0. You see the running total in the desk every day.

Who pays the 1% on top-ups?

You do, only on top-ups jumpers make in the Manifest app. It is never added to the jumper. Cash and your own card terminal have no fee from us.

Is there a contract?

No. No monthly fee, no subscription and no lock-in. You can stop anytime.

How do we switch?

We move your jumpers and balances over for free. Set it up on your own or with us, and run it next to your current system for as long as you like.

What if the internet drops?

The desk keeps working. Check-ins, load calls, boarding and departures are saved and sent the moment the internet is back. Payments and new bookings wait for the connection.

What hardware do we need?

Any laptop or tablet with a browser runs the desk. Any TV with a browser shows the hangar board.

Do jumpers pay for the app?

No. The Manifest app is free to download. It is on iPhone today, and Android is being built.
