Got questions?

What does the Manifest skydiving app do?

Manifest is the app for skydivers, on iPhone. It shows the loads turning live at 150 dropzones, records your jump from your pocket and replays the whole flight in 3D over real terrain, and keeps every jump, signed off in person by licensed jumpers. It also has weather and winds for jumping, gear and reserve repack reminders, your Check-in Pass, dropzones, events, coaches and community.

Is Manifest free?

Manifest is free to download, with no limit on how many jumps you log. Logging, getting jumps signed off, your Check-in Pass, live loads, gear service reminders, dropzones, events and community are all free, and so is recording a jump and watching it replayed in 3D.

Manifest Pro adds:

  • your load on your Lock Screen with gear-up and final-call notifications and a live countdown in your Dynamic Island
  • a recorder that arms itself when your load takes off
  • replay stats
  • two of your jumps on one replay
  • cloud backup of every recording
  • Google Earth and CSV exports of any jump
  • Your day, pre-filled: jumps from your loads arrive ready to confirm
  • branded PDFs of your jumps, tunnel time and gear
  • your jump record and credentials on your Check-in Pass
  • a gold verified check next to your name once your license is verified

Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription, billed by Apple.

Can I replay my skydive in 3D?

Yes, and it is free. Record your jump with your phone in your pocket and Manifest replays the whole flight in 3D over real terrain, from the climb to the landing, using the phone's own GPS and barometer. No extra hardware is needed.

Does it work at my dropzone?

Logging, verification, your pass and your gear work anywhere in the world. Live loads currently work at 150 dropzones, and we are adding more. If yours is not covered yet, everything else still works normally.

Which dropzones does Manifest show live loads for?

Manifest shows live loads from 150 dropzones: what's on call, who's on it and the minutes to call, straight off each dropzone's own manifest. At dropzones that run Manifest Desk, every load call also goes straight to your phone.

Is Android coming?

Yes. Android is being built right now. Email me and I will let you know the day it is out.

Can I bring my existing jumps across?

Yes. You can import your whole history from a CSV, whether it comes from another app or your own spreadsheet. Tunnel time and reserve repacks can be imported too.

How does a jump get verified?

Another licensed jumper who was there scans your QR and signs it off. Their name and licence number are recorded on that jump, so it is witnessed rather than self-reported. Your total jumps and your verified jumps sit side by side.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. You can log jumps with no signal at all and they sync as soon as you are back online, which matters on a dropzone with bad wifi.

Will a dropzone accept it?

Your Check-in Pass shows your licence, currency, jump numbers and gear service in one scannable view, pulled straight from your record so it cannot be edited by hand. The person scanning it does not need the app. Pro also gives you a branded PDF of every signed-off jump.

Is my data private?

Your jumps, credentials and documents are yours and are access-controlled. A dropzone sees your licence, membership, insurance and reserve card only after you share them, never your medical papers or passport, and you can stop sharing at any time. You choose what is public on your profile, you can go fully private, and you can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

What is Manifest Desk?

Manifest Desk is skydiving software for dropzones, running in the browser. It handles loads and exit order, standby, jumper accounts and payments, waivers, staff, aircraft, safety checks and a hangar TV board, and sends every load call to jumpers' phones. Jumpers top up with Apple Pay, straight into the dropzone's own Stripe account. It is free until you fly: no monthly fee, $1 per seat flown plus 1% on top-ups jumpers make in the app, and you can stop anytime. See Manifest Desk or read what our dropzone software does.

Is Manifest listed by USPA?

Yes. Manifest is on the United States Parachute Association's jump log apps page, which lists the digital logbooks that meet USPA's criteria. Every jump records the fields USPA SIM 3-1 requires, and sign-off is in person by another licensed jumper whose licence number goes on the entry.
