One system for both sides of the hangar.

Manifest is the skydiving app jumpers use for everything on a jump day. Manifest Desk is the manifest software that runs the dropzone. They are connected live, so a load called at the desk lands on every jumper's phone.

Why Manifest exists

A jump day shouldn't live on a whiteboard.

Loads go up on a board and get called over the PA. Logbooks are paper nobody can check. The desk runs software jumpers never see. Manifest puts the jumper and the dropzone on one system.

The old way

  • Loads on a whiteboard, calls over the PA
  • A paper logbook nobody can check
  • Repack dates you find out about at the desk
  • A queue at the card terminal
  • A desk your jumpers never see

Manifest

  • Your load and every call on your phone
  • Jumps signed off by coaches and instructors
  • A reminder before your reserve or AAD falls due
  • Apple Pay top-ups in the app
  • A desk connected to the app jumpers carry

For jumpers

Everything on a jump day, in one app.

Check your load, jump, watch it back, get it signed off. Manifest is free to download for iPhone.

Every jump flown back in 3D from your phone's GPS. No extra hardware.

Gear locker

Rigs, reserves and AADs, with a reminder before a repack or service falls due.

Currency and weather

Know you're current, and see the winds on the ground and aloft at your dropzone.

Coaches, people and events

Find a coach, message the jumpers you fly with, see what is on at the dropzone.

For dropzones

Manifest Desk runs the dropzone.

Manifest software for the whole day, from the first load to the last, connected to the app your jumpers already carry.

Loads and calls

Build loads and call them. Every call reaches every phone on the load, and the hangar board shows the rest.

Self-manifest and top-ups

Jumpers book themselves and top up with Apple Pay. The money goes to your dropzone's own Stripe.

Safety

Currency, reserve and AAD checked at manifest. A landout shows up on your map.

Staff and pay

Roles, jobs and pay per jump or per load, worked out from what actually flew.

How we work.

  1. 1Built with the people who use it

    Sign in with Apple, the dropzone search and the way jumps get captured after a load all came from jumpers telling us what was missing.

  2. 2Shipped fast

    New features ship constantly. Tell us what is missing or broken and it gets looked at properly.

  3. 3Your data stays yours

    We do not sell your personal data. Delete a jump or your whole account in the app, any time. Dropzone money goes straight to the dropzone's own Stripe.

  4. 4Set up with you, directly

    Every dropzone is set up with us directly, and we are there on its first jump day. Jumpers write to André, not a ticket queue.
