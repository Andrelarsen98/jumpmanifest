Why Manifest exists

A jump day shouldn't live on a whiteboard.

Loads go up on a board and get called over the PA. Logbooks are paper nobody can check. The desk runs software jumpers never see. Manifest puts the jumper and the dropzone on one system.

The old way

  • Loads on a whiteboard, calls over the PA
  • Paper records nobody can verify
  • Repack dates you find out about at the desk
  • A queue at the card terminal
  • A desk your jumpers never see

Manifest

  • Your load and every call on your phone
  • Every jump recorded and verified
  • A reminder before your reserve or AAD falls due
  • Apple Pay top-ups in the app
  • A desk connected to the app jumpers carry

How we work.

  1. Precision-built

    Software for modern dropzones and the skydivers who fly them, held to the standard of the best apps you use every day.

  2. Always improving

    Regular updates to the app and Manifest Desk, each one tested before it reaches you.

  3. Your data stays yours

    We do not sell your personal data. Delete a jump or your whole account in the app, any time. Dropzone money goes straight to the dropzone's own Stripe.

  4. Set up with you, directly

    Every dropzone is set up with us directly, and we are there on its first jump day. Jumpers write to André, the founder, not a ticket queue.
