A jump day, step by step

How Manifest works at your dropzone.

One ordinary day at a Desk dropzone, on both sides at once.

The appManifest Desk

  1. On the drive in

    AppThe loads are already live.

    Which aircraft, the seats taken, who is on each load and the minutes to call. Live loads run at 150 dropzones.

  2. Getting on a load

    AppBook yourself on.

    Bring your friends with you. If the load is full, join standby. The dropzone sets the limits.

    DeskYour name lands on the load.

    The moment you book. Staff approve you, move you to another load or put you back on standby. The desk always has the final say.

  3. Low on jump tickets

    AppTop up with Apple Pay.

    Or a card, and Cash App Pay in the US. The money goes straight to the dropzone’s own Stripe account.

    DeskThe balance is already there.

    Your new balance shows on your account right away. The dropzone is the merchant, not us.

  4. Load call

    AppEvery phone updates.

    Called, 10 minutes, 5 minutes, boarding and holds, on every jumper’s phone and Lock Screen.

    DeskStaff press one button.

    No paper slips, no shouting across the hangar. A hold on the desk is a hold in every pocket.

  5. In the air

    AppRecording starts before takeoff.

    With Manifest Pro it arms itself 2 minutes before takeoff. Without Pro, tap Record before you board.

    DeskSees nothing during the jump.

    The dropzone does not track you in the air. Your recording stays on your phone.

  6. After landing

    AppWatch the jump in 3D.

    The whole flight over real terrain, from the climb to the landing.

    DeskSees who is back.

    Every name on the load. Staff tick each jumper in as they land.

  7. If someone lands out

    AppOne tap: Landed out?

    In the app or from the Lock Screen, then injured, need a ride, or walking back. The jumper sees who is driving and the ETA.

    DeskOn a live satellite map.

    With the distance from the dropzone until it is resolved. Send a driver a pickup pin, call the jumper, alert chosen staff or hold every load in one tap.

Questions

What happens if the internet drops at our dropzone?

The desk keeps working for the things that cannot wait. It keeps showing the last loads it had, and check-ins, load calls, boarding and departures you make are saved in the browser and sent the moment the internet is back. Payments and new bookings wait for the connection, and a load call whose time has passed is not sent late.

What is the difference between the Manifest app and Manifest Desk?

The app is for jumpers, on iPhone: live loads, self-manifest, top-ups, load calls, recording, the 3D replay and your logbook. Manifest Desk is for dropzone owners and manifest staff, in the browser: the loads board, standby, accounts, staff, aircraft, safety and reports. At a Desk dropzone the two are connected and update each other live.

Does my dropzone need Manifest Desk for me to use the app?

No. Live loads, recording, the 3D replay, the logbook, weather and gear reminders work at any dropzone. Self-manifest, in-app top-ups, one-button load calls and the landout map need your dropzone to run Manifest Desk.

Where does my money go when I top up?

Straight to the dropzone's own Stripe account. The dropzone is the merchant. Your new balance shows on the desk immediately, so you can manifest right away.

Does Manifest track me during the jump?

The dropzone does not see you during the jump. Your own recording stays on your phone for your replay. The desk only sees a position if you tap Landed out after landing, and it stops once the landout is resolved. It supports the dropzone's emergency procedures and depends on battery, GPS and signal, so it is not a guaranteed rescue tracker.

Is there an Android app?

Manifest is on iPhone today. Android is in the making. Tell me the day it is out. Manifest Desk runs in any browser.
